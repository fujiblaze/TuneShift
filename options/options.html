TuneShift controls a tab after you invoke it. On open.spotify.com, a local compatibility hook runs at page load to detect detached media players. Preferences, Quick profiles, saved page and track profiles, and BPM data stay in Chrome's local extension storage. Tab effects capture audio only when you enable them.
